# 🚍 Analyzing Student Experiences with Windsor Bus Services 🚍 

I created a dashboard after my team and I conducted a survey asking students about their experience with the Windsor bus services. Through great teamwork, we were able to collect over 50 responses in a few days. 

One surprising finding was that although more people preferred owning a car over riding the bus, the margin was not as large as I expected. The preference split was 59.2% for owning a car and 40.8% for riding the bus. This highlights an important point: for many students, cars might not be the most economical option during this phase of their lives. 

📊 **Key Takeaway**: 
Data visuals challenge our preconceived notions and tell the story as it is, not as we think it should be.
